## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an editorial, student-friendly Indonesian artisan café identity. It balances refined specialty-roaster craft with approachable warmth and studious comfort. The aesthetic draws heavily from contemporary indie print magazines, botanical greenhouse packaging, and minimalist editorial menus.

- **Atmosphere:** Earthy, slow-paced, aromatic, and grounding. It feels like an open-air pavilion surrounded by tropical monstera leaves, filled with the aroma of pour-overs and pandan canelés.
- **Visual Style:** Editorial Modern Folk. Clean typography pairings meet strict border architectures, botanical framing rules, subtle paper-grade borders, and solid flat offset paper shadows. No glossy blur, no neo-brutalist acid tones, and strictly zero violet or electric gradients.
- **Accents & Artifacts:** Editorial numbering (e.g., `01 — RACIKAN KOPI`), crisp single-weight hairline rules, micro-monospaced receipt badges, and intentional stamp-like stickers rotated between `-2deg` and `2.5deg` to evoke physical paper packaging.

## Colors
The palette evokes lush botanical coffee farms, warm crema, raw can sugar, and creamy oat milk.

- **Primary (`#12301F` - Deep Forest Green):** Used for solid brand header blocks, deep container backgrounds, dominant editorial typography, and high-impact framing borders.
- **Canvas & Surface (`#F3F6EF` - Milky Cream):** The foundational tactile background. Replaces stark digital whites with an unbleached paper and rich milk undertone.
- **Dark Surface Base (`#0B1D13` - Deep Canopy Dark):** Used for nighttime or dark mode viewports, footers, receipt backdrops, and inverted visual blocks.
- **Primary CTA & Accent (`#F2A93B` - Warm Amber Gold):** The main transactional callout. Applied to primary ordering buttons, active tabs, floating action triggers, and highlights.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - **Strawberry Berry (`#C4325B`):** Seasonal promotions, pastry tags, and critical alert states.
  - **Latte Roast Brown (`#8A5A37`):** Origin stories, roast profile indicators, and secondary tags.
  - **Sprout Leaf Green (`#7CC08A`):** Availability badges, dietary certifications (vegan, organic), and status pips.
- **Text & Contrast:**
  - **Primary Ink (`#1D2A21`):** Applied to headlines, item titles, and primary data.
  - **Muted Ink (`#52645A`):** Applied to ingredient lists, descriptors, and secondary metadata.
  - **Border Stroke (`#E1E7DA`):** Low-contrast structural 1px rule applied across surfaces.

## Typography
Typography creates an editorial structure with three distinct type roles:

1. **Headings:** Set in editorial serif in uppercase or sentence case with tight tracking and a firm weight. Headlines command the rhythm of the layout like section titles in a literary food journal.
2. **Body:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans to maintain effortless legibility for tasting notes, coffee origins, and WiFi work-friendly guidelines.
3. **Labels & Metadata:** Set in monospaced uppercase (12px, tracking `0.08em`) for section enumerations (`01 — ROAST & BREW`), order numbers, roast levels, botanical cultivar notes, and currency markers (`IDR 38K`).

## Layout & Spacing
- **Layout Architecture:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop, transitioning to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Rhythm:** Layouts favor generous breathing room between sections (`space-xl` to `4rem`), bordered by crisp 1px dividing lines reminiscent of newspaper margins.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 16px margins, single-column product flows, sticky bottom order bar.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 24px margins, alternating two-column layouts.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** Max container width `1280px`, wide asymmetric margins, multi-pane menu configurations with sticky table-side order sidebars.

## Elevation & Depth
This system completely rejects blurry drop shadows, colored ambient glows, and skeuomorphic gradients in favor of tactile paper layering.

- **Flat Hard Offset Shadow:** Highlighted cards, seasonal feature callouts, and active dialog modals use a solid, zero-blur drop shadow: `4px 4px 0px #12301F`.
- **Primary Buttons Hover State:** Translates `-2px -2px` with an expanded shadow of `4px 4px 0px #12301F`. On active press, elements depress to `0px 0px 0px #12301F` with a translation of `+2px +2px`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Standard surfaces sit on top of the milky cream canvas separated purely by a crisp `1px solid #12301F` (for high emphasis) or `1px solid #E1E7DA` (for subtle containment).
- **Z-Index Tiers:**
  - Base canvas: `#F3F6EF`
  - Floating items / Sticker stamps: Rotated layers floating with `4px 4px 0px rgba(18, 48, 31, 0.15)`
  - Modals & Drawer Overlays: Framed in solid forest green hairlines over a solid 40% tinted forest wash.

## Shapes
The design system enforces a strict three-tier radius rule:

1. **`4px` (Structural Micro-Radius):** Used for interactive cards, quantity counters, input text fields, and thumbnail frames. Gives a clean, lightly softened paper cut edge without looking round.
2. **`16px` (Card & Panel Radius):** Used exclusively for featured content containers, bottom sheets, order summary drawers, and dietary badge groupings.
3. **`9999px` (Full Pill):** Reserved exclusively for pills, action buttons, filter chips, and ingredient tags.
- **Forbidden:** Radii of 6px, 8px, 12px, 20px, or 24px are strictly prohibited to maintain clear visual cadence.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#F2A93B` (Warm Amber Gold), text `#1D2A21`, border `1px solid #12301F`, shape `9999px` full pill. Typography: monospaced 12px bold uppercase with `0.08em` letter-spacing. Padding: `12px 24px`. Flat shadow: `4px 4px 0px #12301F`.
- **Secondary / Botanical Button:** Background `#12301F` (Forest Green), text `#F3F6EF` (Milky Cream), border `1px solid #12301F`, shape `9999px`. Shadow: `4px 4px 0px #F2A93B`.
- **Ghost Button:** Background transparent, text `#12301F`, border `1px solid #12301F`, shape `9999px`. Hover fills with `rgba(18, 48, 31, 0.06)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Filter Chips:** Shape `9999px`, border `1px solid #12301F`. Default state: background `#F3F6EF`, text `#52645A`. Active state: background `#12301F`, text `#F3F6EF`.
- **Editorial Origin Badges:** Shape `4px`, background `#F3F6EF`, border `1px solid #E1E7DA`. Typography: `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase. Examples: `[WEST JAVA - GUNTUR]`, `[ANAEROBIC NATURAL]`.
- **Sticker Badges:** Small floating stamps (e.g., `BARU`, `LIMITED BEANS`) with background `#C4325B` or `#F2A93B`, border `1px solid #12301F`, rotated `-2deg` or `+2deg`.

### Menu Cards & Lists
- **Standard Menu List Row:** Border-bottom `1px solid #E1E7DA`. Title in Newsreader serif 18px, single-line dotted leader connecting to the monospaced price (`35K`), with tasting notes in Plus Jakarta Sans 13px muted gray-green.
- **Featured Dish/Drink Card:** Surface `#FFFFFF` or `#F9FAF6`, border `1px solid #12301F`, radius `16px`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #12301F`. Contains product photography framed with a `4px` inner radius and a 1px border.

### Input Fields
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #12301F`, radius `4px`.
- Padding `14px 16px`.
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans 14px. Focus state: creates a solid `2px 2px 0px #12301F` offset accent.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Checkbox:** `18px x 18px`, `4px` corner radius, border `1px solid #12301F`. Checked fill: `#12301F` with a white custom hairline tick.
- **Radio Button:** `18px x 18px`, fully circular (`9999px`), border `1px solid #12301F`. Selected state contains an inner dot of `#F2A93B` surrounded by `#12301F`.

### Iconography & Section Numbering
- **Icons:** Thin 1.5px stroke geometric icons matching `#12301F`. Emojis are strictly banned from UI labels.
- **Section Headers:** Prefixed with sequential numerals (`01 — MANUAL BREW`, `02 — ARTISAN PASTRY`, `03 — WORKSPACE & PASS`).
